TangleGuard Designer is a desktop app available for all major operating systems.

It allows you to **browse any local codebase visually** as dependency graph.

<LinkCard 
  title="Exploration Guide"
  href="/designer/exploration/"
  description="See how to browse the codebase and the insights you get."
/>

In addition you can define your *target architecture* in form of dependency rules.
Those rules are basically **architectural lints**. 
The app validates the codebase against those rules/lints. 
As a result, you get a list of violations, as well is a graph diagram showing the violated dependencies.
