## Evolution Tracking

The workspace abstraction mentioned in [here](/features/foundation) contains crucial information about the codebase.
The metrics are calculated based on that.

TangleGuard wants to offer a way to track the evolution of the codebase over time.

<Aside>
  Not yet supported. Contact us to speed up the development at
  [kontakt@jaads.de](mailto:kontakt@jaads.de).
</Aside>

The idea is to have a dashboard that shows trends of the metrics.
By looking at the last months or years, you would get an understanding of whether the codebase is getting more tangled, or more structured,
of if it is growing or shrinking, etc.
